Jewby

Share culture-inspired products with your audience

Jewby is a desktop publishing tool for creators and small brands that sell everyday items inspired by traditional Chinese culture—such as Chinese-style phone cases and Zhuang brocade pet accessories. Connect your short-video account, choose local videos, and upload them to finish posting on the platform.

What Jewby does

One focused workflow: authorize, select media, upload, and confirm status.

  • Secure account connection Sign in with your short-video account through official OAuth so Jewby only acts with your permission.
  • Local video upload Pick product videos from your computer, add a caption, and send them through the Content Posting upload flow.
  • Publish status feedback Track whether an upload succeeded so you can review results before continuing your content work.

The Jewby desktop app

Jewby runs as a local desktop web app. Creators authorize once, then upload product videos from a folder on their computer and review upload results in the same interface.

The Jewby app icon above is the same mark used in Basic Info and as this site’s favicon.

Who it’s for

Jewby is built for independent makers, culture-product sellers, and small creative teams that already shoot product clips and want a simple desktop path to upload those clips to their authorized short-video profile.

How it works

  1. Open the Jewby desktop app on your computer.
  2. Click authorize and complete the official login / QR consent screen.
  3. Choose a local product video and enter an optional caption.
  4. Upload the video, then check status in the app until the transfer completes.
  5. Finish any remaining edits or posting steps inside the short-video app if required.
