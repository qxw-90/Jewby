GSByTikTok

GSByTikTok is a desktop tool that helps creators share thoughtfully designed products inspired by traditional Chinese culture on TikTok—such as Chinese-style phone cases and Zhuang brocade pet accessories—and review feedback on published content.

After you sign in with TikTok, the app can publish videos from a folder you choose, check publishing status, and show the results.
