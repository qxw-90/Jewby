Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026 · Last updated: July 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GSByTikTok (“App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use our desktop application to authorize a TikTok account, publish product videos, and review publishing results.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the GSByTikTok desktop app and the official legal pages hosted for the project. It does not control TikTok’s own products. TikTok processes data under its separate privacy policy when you use TikTok services.

2. Information We Access or Process

Depending on how you use the App, we may process the following categories of information:

  • TikTok account basics (via user.info.basic): information needed to identify the connected account, such as display name and avatar, so you can confirm where videos will be published.
  • TikTok account stats (via user.info.stats): basic public account statistics (for example follower or video counts) shown in the App to help you review account context around shared content.
  • Video list data (via video.list): recent video information used to confirm publications, avoid duplicate posts, and display status/results.
  • Video upload/publish data (via video.upload / Content Posting API): the local video file you select and related publish metadata needed to submit and track a posting request.
  • Local files you select: videos remain on your device until you choose to upload/publish them through the App.
  • Comment-region feedback (limited): where available for analyzing product feedback on videos we publish, we may collect only the regional location associated with users who comment on those videos. We do not collect other personal or private commenter information for this purpose.

3. How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Authenticate you with TikTok and maintain your connected-session workflow;
  • Publish selected local videos to your authorized TikTok account;
  • Check publish status and show results in the App;
  • Help you understand audience response to shared product videos through limited regional feedback analysis;
  • Improve reliability, security, and support for the App.

We do not sell personal information and do not use TikTok data for unrelated advertising profiles.

4. Legal Basis / Permission Model

Access to TikTok account and video data occurs only after you explicitly authorize the App through TikTok’s OAuth consent screen. Publishing actions occur only when you initiate them in the App.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information only as needed to operate the App:

  • TikTok: to authenticate, upload/publish videos, and retrieve status or related API responses;
  • Service providers (if any are later used for hosting or support), solely to operate the App under appropriate safeguards;
  • Legal requirements: if disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or integrity.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including to complete publish workflows, show recent results, perform limited feedback analysis, troubleshoot issues, or meet legal obligations. You can disconnect TikTok authorization to stop future API access.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect authorization tokens and related data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may decline TikTok authorization or revoke it later in TikTok settings.
  • You control which local videos are selected for publishing.
  • You may stop using the App at any time.
  • Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain information. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children and is intended for users who are allowed to use TikTok under applicable age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International Processing

Information may be processed in countries where you, TikTok, or our infrastructure providers operate. Those locations may have different data-protection laws from your home country.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be submitted through the project repository: github.com/qxw-90/GSByTikTok/issues.
