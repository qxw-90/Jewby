Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026 · Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jewby (“App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use our desktop application to authorize a short-video account, upload product videos, and review upload results.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Jewby desktop app and the official website / legal pages hosted for the product. It does not control the short-video platform’s own products. That platform processes data under its separate privacy policy when you use its services.

2. Information We Access or Process

Depending on how you use the App, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Account basics (via user.info.basic): information needed to identify the connected account, such as display name and avatar, so you can confirm where videos will be uploaded.
  • Video upload data (via video.upload / Content Posting API): the local video file you select and related upload metadata needed to submit and track an upload request.
  • Local files you select: videos remain on your device until you choose to upload them through the App.

3. How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Authenticate you with the connected platform and maintain your connected-session workflow;
  • Upload selected local videos to your authorized account;
  • Check upload status and show results in the App;
  • Improve reliability, security, and support for the App.

We do not sell personal information and do not use platform data for unrelated advertising profiles.

4. Legal Basis / Permission Model

Access to account and upload capabilities occurs only after you explicitly authorize the App through the platform’s OAuth consent screen. Upload actions occur only when you initiate them in the App.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information only as needed to operate the App:

  • Connected short-video platform: to authenticate, upload videos, and retrieve status or related API responses;
  • Service providers (if any are later used for hosting or support), solely to operate the App under appropriate safeguards;
  • Legal requirements: if disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or integrity.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including to complete upload workflows, show recent results, troubleshoot issues, or meet legal obligations. You can disconnect platform authorization to stop future API access.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect authorization tokens and related data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may decline authorization or revoke it later in the platform’s settings.
  • You control which local videos are selected for upload.
  • You may stop using the App at any time.
  • Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain information. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children and is intended for users who meet the connected platform’s applicable age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International Processing

Information may be processed in countries where you, the connected platform, or our infrastructure providers operate. Those locations may have different data-protection laws from your home country.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: jewby.support@gmail.com or github.com/qxw-90/jewby/issues.
